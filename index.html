Who We Are

A New York Institution

Born in the heart of Manhattan, built on the belief that great pizza should be fast, fresh, and totally yours.

Est. 2019 · Midtown Manhattan

Empire Slice Co. was founded with one simple idea: New Yorkers deserve a pizza experience that moves as fast as they do. We opened our doors in 2019 on West 47th Street and haven't looked back since.

We specialize in fully customizable pies and slices — choose your dough, sauce, cheese, and load up with over 30 fresh toppings. Whether you're a traditionalist or an adventurous eater, we've got a slice with your name on it.

Our cheese is sourced exclusively from Galbani, Italy's most iconic dairy brand — because a great pizza starts with great mozzarella. Every pie is fired in our stone deck ovens and ready in under five minutes.
